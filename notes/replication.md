Why the all-layer diff-of-means ablations break Phi: the raw-space diff-of-means directions (state, C1vC4-dm,
C1vC5-last-dm) put large weight (|component| up to 0.5–0.6) on Phi's few massive-activation dimensions (1113, 2046,
2024, 2812; e.g. |μ| at L24 is 266, of which ~144 sits on each of dims 2024/2812). Projecting those directions out at
every position (including the attention-sink first token) destroys generation: outputs become garbage such as
`EEEpe\nFMFEclj…`, and ARC drops to 0. The LR (w/σ) direction has ≈0 weight on those dimensions because σ is huge
there, and random directions do not concentrate on them, so both are harmless. On Phi, therefore, the all-layer
positive control is not interpretable. The pre-registered spec was all layers; the layer-restricted (L16–31 = the
state-decodable layers) variant is a post-hoc addition and is labelled as such.

### Readings
- **Phi: the late-layer state ablation is specific to covert decoding**, as on the 7B. Covert collusion drops to
  chance (every C1 episode decoded COLD), while explicit-state betting is untouched (C6 1.000), and ARC 0.99 and
  recall 0.94 hold. **But the variance-matched PC control (smaller variance: 14% vs 33%) also kills covert decoding**
  (bet_exact 0.408, pushing to the NEUTRAL default) and dents explicit betting (C6 0.843). So on Phi the state
  subspace is distinguished from a generic high-variance subspace by its *selectivity* (C6 intact, COLD default),
  not by being uniquely necessary for C1. This is the same pattern as the 7B (PC 0.60 / C6 0.80), only stronger.
- **14B: the all-layer state ablation is not specific.** It also damages explicit betting (C6 0.712), recall
  (0.75) and ARC (0.82), and a lower-variance PC control produces the same covert-collusion loss (C1 bet_exact 0.627
